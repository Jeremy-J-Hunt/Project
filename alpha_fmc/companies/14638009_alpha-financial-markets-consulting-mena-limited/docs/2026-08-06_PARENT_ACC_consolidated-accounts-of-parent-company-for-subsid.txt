Impairment review

The carrying amount of goodwill ts not subject to systematic amortisation but is instead reviewed
annually for impairment The review assesses each CGU to which goodwill has been allocated by
comparing the carrying amount of the CGU, including goodwill, with the recoverable amount of the
CGU Deferred tax liabilities arising on acquired intangible assets are included within the carrying
amount of each CGU for the purposes of the impairment review Including these deferred tax liabilities
within the CGU carrying amount ts consistent with the treatment of goodwill arising on acquisition
under IFRS 3 Business Combinations and avoids a notional impairment immediately following
acquisition that does not reflect a change In the recoverable amount of the CGUs

The recoverable amount of each CGU has been assessed by reference to value in use, which Is
estimated using cash flows from torecasts prepared by management around the balance sheet date
The key assumptions considered to be individually significant for the purposes of calculating the value
in use of each CGU include future growth rates, profit margins and the pre-tax discount rates used to
convert the estimated cash flows to present value

The board-approved budget for the year ended 31 March 2027 forms the basis for the cash flow
projections for each CGU The cash flow projections for the following four years reflect the directors’
expectations of the medium-term operating performance of the CGU and the growth prospects in the
relevant market The factors considered in relation to each CGU include the size of the CGU, the
maturity of its operations, its market share and the Group's strategic pillars Thereafter, a long-term
growth rate is applied to the terminal penod, reflecting the lang-term outlook for each CGU

To determine the recoverable amount of each CGU, future cash flows have been discounted to their
present value using CGU-specific pre-tax discount rates These rates reflect current market
assessments of the time value of money and the risks specific to each CGU, including the geographic
regions in which the CGU operates and the nature, matunty, and stability of its operations

The cash flow projections are stated on a nominal basis, incorporating expected inflationary effects
over the forecast period to ensure consistency with the nominal discount rates applied The Group
has also considered costs in relation to climate change when forecasting future cash flows

Sensitivity analysis

Given the level of goodwill recognised in the consolidated balance, the impairment review 1s sensitive
to reasonably possible changes tn the key assumptions used in determining the recoverable amount
of each CGU The key assumptions are as follows

° Medium-term growth rate the rate at which adjusted earnings before interest, tax,
depreciation and amortisation (‘EBITDA’) is expected to grow in years two to five of the
forecast period

° Long-term growth rate the rate at which adjusted EBITDA Is assumed to grow in perpetuity,
used to determine the terminal value

* Pre-tax discount rate the rate used to discount forecast cash flows to present value,
teflecting a market participant's view of the weighted average cost of capital
