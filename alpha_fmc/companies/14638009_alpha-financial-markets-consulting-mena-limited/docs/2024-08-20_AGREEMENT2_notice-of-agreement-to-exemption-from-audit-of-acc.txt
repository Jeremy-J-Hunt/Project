Exemption from Audit by Parent Guarantee

According to the UK Companies Act 2006 Section 479, a company is exempt from the
requirements of the Act relating to the audit of its individual accounts (but not any group accounts)
for a financial year if the company is included in the consolidated accounts drawn up for that year,
or to an earlier year in that year, by the parent undertaking in accordance with the UK-adopted
international accounting standards.
