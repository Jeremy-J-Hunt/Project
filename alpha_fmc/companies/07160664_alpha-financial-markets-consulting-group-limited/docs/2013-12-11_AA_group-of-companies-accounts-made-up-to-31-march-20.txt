FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 MARCH 2013

The directors present their report and the audited accounts of the group for the year ended 31 March 2013

Principal activities and review of the business
The principal activity of the company continued to be that of a holding company The principal activities of the subsidiary
undertakings are the provision of consulting and related services to clients in the asset and wealth management industries

The group made strong progress during the year Turnover increased 38% from £10 2 million to £14 2 million and operating
profit increased 38% from £1 3 million to £18 million All four offices, Lendon, Paris, Luxembourg and New York contributed
to this growth Debtors have grown considerably during the year from £3 4 miltion to £6 million reflecting an increase in the
working capital requirement due to revenue growth and a particularly strong final quarter There are no concerns over the
recoverability of debtors

Results and dividends
The results of the group for the year are set out on page 4

An interim ordinary dividend was paid amounting to £424,957 (2012 ENiI) The directors do not recommend payment of a
final dividend (2012 ENil)

After deducting taxation and dividends, the amount of £766,940 (2012 £751,286 as restated) has been transferred to
reserves:

Future developments

The business pian developed by the directors shows substantial growth 1s expected in the business in the forthcoming year
The directors are confident of achieving their plan based on the level of on-going work at the start of the financial year, the
level of work already sold but still to be delivered and the strong pipeline of future opportunities These indicators are at
historically high levels for the group

Key performance indicators

The directors monitor performance on both a weekly and a monthly basis using a range of financial and non financial
indicators Working capital management is the primary indicator which 1s reviewed on a weekly basis to monitor cashflow,
liquidity and the recovery of trade debtors

Each month the directors review the management accounts for each group undertaking and compare actual performance
against budget Costs are also reviewed to monitor profitability

Principal risks

The performance of the group 1s subject to only limited nsk in the short and medium term as the future pipeline of work !s

4 Accounting Policies

Accounting convention
The accounts are prepared under the historical cost convention

Changes n accounting policies

The group has adopted FRS 20 ‘Share-Based Payment’, owing to the fact that it 1s no longer eligible to apply the
Financial Reporting Standard for Smaller Entities (FRSSE) The adoption of this standard represents a change in
accounting policy and the comparative figures have been restated accordingly as descnbed in more detail in note 24

Compliance with accounting standards
The accounts are prepared in accordance with applicable United Kingdom Accounting Standards (United Kingdom
Generally Accepted Accounting Practice), which have been applied consistently (except as otherwise stated)

Basis of consolidation
The consolidated accounts include the results of the company and Its group undertakings No profit and loss account
Is presented for the company as permitted by section 408 of the Companies Act 2006

Turnover
Turnover represents amounts recetvable for services net of VAT

Depreciation
Tangible fixed assets are stated at cost less depreciation Depreciation Is provided at rates calculated to write off the
cost less estimated residual value of each asset over its expected useful life, as follows

Leasehold improvements over the lease term
Computer equipment 33% on cost
Fixtures, fittings and equipment 25% on cost
Goodwill

Goodwill arising on acquisitions 1s written off in equal annual instalments over the directors’ estimate of its useful
economic iife of 20 years .

Leasing
Rentals payable under operating leases are charged against income on a straight line basis over the lease term

investments
Fixed asset investments are stated at cost less provision for diminution in value

Pensions
The group operates a defined contribution scheme for the benefit of its employees Contributions payable are charged
to the profit and loss account in the year they are payable

Deferred taxation
Deferred taxation is provided in respect of material taxation deferred by timing differences between the treatment of
certain items for taxation and accounting purposes The deferred tax balance has not been discounted

Foreign currency translation

Monetary assets and liabilities denominated in foreign currencies are translated into sterling at the rates of exchange
Tuling at the balance sheet date Transactions in foreign currencies are recorded at the rate ruling at the date of the
transaction All differences are taken to the profit and loss account

Share-based payments

The group has issued share options to certain directors and employees These must be measured at fair value and
recognised as an expense tn the profit and loss account with a corresponding increase In equity The fair value of the
options 1s estimated at the date of grant using the Black-Scholes Option Pricing Model The fair value is charged as an
expense in the profit and loss account over the vesting period and adjusted each year to reflect the expected and
actual level of vesting
