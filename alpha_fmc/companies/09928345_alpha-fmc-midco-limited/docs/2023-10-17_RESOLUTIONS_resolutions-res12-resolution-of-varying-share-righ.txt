ORDINARY RESOLUTION

1 That the directors are generally and unconditionally authorised for the purposes of s551 of the
CA 2006 and the articles of association of the Company to exercise all the powers of the Company

shares in the Company up to an aggregate nominal amount of £7.00, provided that:

a. (unless previously revoked, varied or renewed by the Company) this authority will expire on

this authority had not expired; and

purposes of s551, which, to the extent unused at the date of this resolution, are revoked with
immediate effect, without prejudice to any allotment of shares or grant of rights already made,
offered or agreed to be made under such authorities.
