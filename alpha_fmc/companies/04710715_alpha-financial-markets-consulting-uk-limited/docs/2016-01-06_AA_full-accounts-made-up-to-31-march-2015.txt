The directors present their report and the audited financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2015.
Principal activities and review of the business

The principal activity of the company continued to be the provision of consulting and related services to clients in the
asset and wealth management industries.

On 24 October 2013, Alpha FMC Group Limited bought the entire share capital of Alpha Financial Markets
Consulting Group Limited, the parent of Alpha Financial Markets Consulting Limited.

Results and dividends
The results for the year are set out on page 6.

An interim ordinary dividend was paid amounting to £4,850,000 (20/4: £600,000). The directors do not recommend
payment ofa final dividend (2014: £nil).

After deducting taxation, dividends and accounting for share based payments, reserves have been reduced by £781,000
(2014: £3,880,000 transferred to reserves).

Strategic report

Business review

The company continued to perform well during the year, achieving an increase in turnover of 5% from £22,758,000
to £23,797,000. Trade Debtors increased to £4,319,000 from £3,908,000, an increase of 11% due in part to the increase
in revenue and in part to an increase in average invoice value as a result of larger project sizes. There are no concerns
about the recoverability of the main debtors.

Future developments

The business plan developed by the directors shows growth is expected to continue in the forthcoming year. The
directors.are confident of achieving their plan based on the level of on-going work at the start of the financial year,
the level of work already sold but still to be delivered and the strong pipeline of future opportunities.

Key performance indicators

The directors monitor performance on both a weekly and a monthly basis using a range of financial and non-financial
indicators. Working capital management is the primary indicator which is reviewed on a weekly basis to monitor cash
flow, liquidity and the recovery of trade debtors.

Each month the directors review the management accounts for each group undertaking and compare actual
performance against budget. Costs are also reviewed to monitor profitability.
Principal risks

The performance of the company is subject to only limited risk in the short and medium term as the future pipeline of
work is very strong. The company continues to generate substantial cash after retaining a proportion of profits to fund
increased working capital requirements resulting from growth. Management continue to operate conservative cash
management policies.

1 Accounting policies

The following accounting policies have been applied consistently in dealing with items which are considered material
in relation to the Company’s financial statements.

Basis of preparation

The financial statements are prepared in accordance with applicable United Kingdom Accounting Standards (United
Kingdom Generally Accepted Accounting Practice), which have been applied consistently (except as otherwise stated)
and under historical cost accounting rules.

The Company is exempt by virtue of s400 of the Companies Act 2006 from the requirement to prepare group financial
statements as it is included in the consolidated financial statements of Alpha FMC Group Holdings Limited. These
financial statements present information about the Company as an individual undertaking and not about its group.

Under FRS 1, the Company is exempt from the requirement to prepare a cash flow statement on the grounds that a
parent undertaking includes the company in its own published consolidated financial statements.

As the Company is a wholly owned subsidiary of Alpha FMC Group Holdings Limited, the Company has taken
advantage of the exemption contained in FRS 8 and has therefore not disclosed transactions or balances with entities
which form part of the group. The consolidated financial statements of Alpha FMC Group Holdings Limited, within
which this company is included, can be obtained from the address given in note 21.

Turnover
Turnover represents amounts receivable for services, net of VAT. Turnover is recognised upon delivery of the service.
Tangible fixed assets and depreciation

Tangible fixed assets are stated at cost less depreciation. Depreciation is provided at rates calculated to write off the
cost less estimated residual value of each asset over its expected useful life, as follows:

Leasehold Land and buildings over the lease term
Computer equipment 3 years straight line
Fixtures, fittings and equipment 4 years straight line

Investments
Fixed asset investments are stated at cost less provision for diminution in value.
Pensions

The company operates a defined contribution scheme for the benefit of its employees. Contributions payable are
charged to the profit and loss account in the year they are payable.

Taxation

The charge for taxation is based on the profit for the year and takes into account taxation deferred because of timing
differences between the treatment of certain items for taxation and accounting purposes.

Deferred taxation is provided in respect of material taxation deferred by timing differences between the treatment of
certain items for taxation and accounting purposes. The deferred tax balance has not been discounted.

Foreign currency translation

Monetary assets and liabilities denominated in foreign currencies are translated into sterling at the rates of exchange
ruling at the balance sheet date. Transactions in foreign currencies are recorded at the rate ruling at the date of the
transaction. All differences are taken to profit and loss account.

Share-based payments

The company has issued share options to certain directors and employees. These must be measured at fair value and
recognised as an expense in the profit and loss account with a corresponding increase in equity. The fair value of the
options is estimated at the date of grant using the Black-Scholes Option Pricing Model. The fair value is charged as
an expense in the profit and loss account over the vesting period and adjusted each year to reflect the expected and
actual level of vesting.

Dividends on shares presented within shareholders’ funds

appropriately authorised and are no longer at the-discretion of the Company. Unpaid dividends that do not meet these
criteria are disclosed in the notes to the financial statements.

15 Share based payment transactions (continued)

During the prior year, the following share options were exercised: 49,480 Ordinary ‘ANV’ shares of 10p each, with
an estimated weighted average share price at the date of exercise of 10.29p; 24,450 Ordinary ‘A’ shares of 10p each
at a weighted average share price at the date of exercise of £19.81; 3,788 Ordinary ‘UK’ shares of £3.10 each at a
weighted average share price at the date of exercise of £15.93; 23,200 Ordinary ‘C’ shares of 10p each at a weighted
average share price at the date of exercise of 10p.

In accordance with FRS 20 'Share-Based Payments’, the fair value of each of the share options has been calculated by
applying the Black-Scholes Option Pricing Model to the estimated share price.on the grant date of the option.

The model inputs were the relevant share price at grant date, the exercise price as stated in the option agreement,
expected volatility of 25%, no expected dividends, an option life based on the best estimate of the expected vesting
period, and a risk-free interest rate of 3%.

To allow for the effects of early exercise, the option life is based on an estimate of the option's expected life rather
than its contractual life.

Volatility has been determined based on historical volatility in the estimated share price.
No share options were granted in the current or prior year.
