DIRECTORS' REPORT
FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 MARCH 2013

The directors present their report and the audited accounts for the year ended 31 March 2013

Principal activities and review of the business

The principal activity of the company continued to be the provision of consulting and related services to clients
In the asset and wealth management industnes

The company has made strong progress during the year Turnover increased 25% from £9,343,643 to
£11,692,843 and operating profit increased 9% from £1,724,200 to £1,874,109 Debtors have grown
considerably during the year from £2,939,935 to £4,990,341 reflecting an increase in the working capitat
requirement due to revenue growth and a particularly strong final quarter There are no concerns over the
recoverability of debtors

Results and dividends
The results for the year are set out on page 5

An intenm ordinary dividend was paid amounting to £665,000 (2012 £400,000) The directors do not
recommend payment of a final dividend (2012 £Nil)

After deducting taxation and dividends, the amount of £1,475,878 (2012 £926,854 as restated) has been
transferred to reserves

Future developments

The business plan developed by the directors shows substantial growth 1s expected in the company in the
forthcoming year The directors are confident of achieving their plan based on the level of on-going work at
the start of the financial year, the jevel of work already sold but still to be delivered and the strong pipeline of
future opportunities These indicators are at histoncally high levels for the company

Key performance indicators

The directors monitor performance on both a weekly and a monthly basis using a range of financial and non
financial indicators Working capital management is the primary indicator which 1s reviewed on a weekly basis
to monitor cashflow, liquidity and the recovery of trade debtors

Each month the directors review the management accounts for each group undertaking and compare
actual performance against budget Costs are also reviewed to monitor profitability

Principal nsks

The performance of the company ts subject to only limited risk in the short and medium term as the future
pipeline of work is very strong The company continues to generate substantial cash after retaining a
proportion of profits to fund increased working capital requirements resulting from growth Management
continue to operate conservative cash management policies

Accounting policies.

Accounting convention
The accounts are prepared under the historical cost convention

Compliance with accounting standards

The accounts are prepared in accordance with applicable United Kingdom Accounting Standards (United
Kingdom Generally Accepted Accounting Practice), which have been applied consistently (except as
otherwise stated)

Turnover
Turnover represents amounts receivable for services net of VAT

Tangible fixed assets and depreciation

Tangibie fixed assets are stated at cost less depreciation Depreciation is provided at rates calculated to
write off the cost less estimated residual value of each asset over tts expected useful life, as follows

Leasehold Land and buildings over the lease term
Computer equipment 3 years straight line
Fixtures, fittngs and equipment 4 years straight line
investments

Fixed asset investments are stated at cost less provision for diminution in value

Pensions
The company operates a defined contribution scheme for the benefit of its employees Contributions
payable are charged to the profit and loss account in the year they are payable

Deferred taxation

Deferred taxation is provided in respect of material taxation deferred by timing differences between the
treatment of certain ttems for taxation and accounting purposes The deferred tax balance has not been
discounted

Foreign currency translation

Monetary assets and liabilities denominated in foreign currencies are transiated into sterling at the rates of
exchange ruling at the balance sheet date Transactions in foreign currencies are recorded at the rate
tuling at the date of the transaction All differences are taken to profit and loss account

Share-based payments

The company has issued share options to certain directors and employees These must be measured at
fair value and recognised as an expense in the profit and loss account with a corresponding increase in
equity The fair value of the options is estimated at the date of grant using the Black-Scholes Option
Pricing Model The fair value ts charged as an expense in the profit and loss account over the vesting
penod and adjusted each year to reflect the expected and actual tevel of vesting
