Environment

AS a professional services business, the Group's direct environmental footprint remains relatively low
Nonetheless, we recognise our responsibility to address climate change and environmental impacts
more broadly, and continue to take practical steps to reduce emissions and embed sustainability
across our operations

In the period, the Group has strengthened the way tt captures and interprets emissions data, enabling
more comprehensive scope 3 reporting and providing a more robust basis for action Rather than
pursuing science-based targets or Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (“TCFD")
alignment at this stage, the priority is to establish a reliable emissions baseline and set out a clear
path towards net zero

The Group continues to reduce its emissions footprint wherever possible — working with landlords for
leased offices to increase renewable energy use, encouraging low-carbon choices around travel, and
introducing improvements in waste management Where emissions cannot yet be avoided, we are
transitioning from traditional offsets towards participation in the voluntary biodiversity and carbon
markets ("BVCM"), aligning this with our broader community and chanty partnerships for long-term,
community-based impact Our commitment to environmental transparency remains strong While we
no longer report via CDP, our EcoVadis submission has now been completed at a global Group level,
with a score pending

In parallel with internal efforts, our Environment, Social & Governance ("ESG"} & Responsible
Investment practice remains a key area of client work Our consultants continue to support financial
services orgamsations in shaping ESG strategies, navigating regulatory developments, and using
