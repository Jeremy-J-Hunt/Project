“that, the issued share capital of the Company be reorganised from £275,000 divided into
275,000 ordinary shares of £1 each to £275,000 divided into 275,000 A Ordinary Shares of
10p each and 8,250,000 B Ordinary Shares of 3p each by subdividing each issued ordinary
share of £1 into 1 “A” Ordinary share of 10p and 30 “B" ordinary Shares of 3p, such A
Ordinary Shares and B Ordinary Shares to rank pari passu in all respects with the
remaining unissued Ordinary £1 shares ”
