2.7 Turnover

Turmover consists of the value of work executed for clients dunng the year and expenses recharged,
exclusive of VAT. Turnover is measured based on the transaction price allocated to the performance
obligation from the sale of goods or provision of services Turnover is recognised either at a ‘point in
time’ when the performance obligation in the contract has been performed or ‘over time’ as control of
the goods or services are transferred to the customer

Turnover generated from software licensing, hosting and related support services provided over a
contracted period of time is combined and allocated to a single performance obiigation These
contracts are considered to be satisfied over time, in line with the benefits being transferred to the
customer Turnover Is recognised on straight-line basis over the contractual license term

Implementation services provided by the Company to the customer are classified as either being
distinct performance obligations or non-distinct from the licensing and hosting promises described
above, dependent on the fact pattern within the underlying contractual arrangement The Company
considers the level of inter-dependency between the contractual promises, and whether the
customer can consume the benefits of the licence without the implementation servica being
performed Where implementation services are required for the customer to use the software, these
services are considered to be non-distinct from the licensing performance obligation Therefore, the
Company accounts for these as a single performance obligation satisfied over the licence term, as
described above The Company generally considers the initial phase of implementation to be non-
distinct from the associated licence

Other distinct implementation services, as well as separately billable maintenance, support, data
assurance and training are treated as separate performance obligations and turnover Is recognised
as these services are delivered

Activity performance recognised as turnover in excess of invaices raised are contract receivables
and are included within accrued income, up to the value of the relevant project delivery milestone,
where applicable On invoicing, the contract receivable is reclassified to trade receivables Where
amounts have been invoiced in excess of turnover recognised, the excess 1s a contract hability and
Is included within deferred income

In relation to licencing contracts, billing typically occurs at the beginning of the contractual Itcence
penod or incrementally over the course of the licence, in line with the contractual terms For
implementation services, billing typically occurs in arrears tn line with a contractually agreed

echedule
