Notes to the Financial Statements

1 Basis of preparation

These interim financial statements of Alpha FMC Topco Limited (the “Company”) have been prepared to provide the
Directors with the financial position of the Company as at 3 October 2017 for the purposes of ensuring sufficient
distributable reserves are available for an interim dividend payment in accordance with sections 836 and 838 of the
Companies Act 2006. These financial statements are abridged and unaudited but have been prepared using accounting
policies consistent with UK accounting standards, including FRS 102 The Financial Reporting Standard applicable in
the UK and Republic of Ireland. ;

These accounts are not the Company’s statutory financial statements. Statutory financial statements for the financial
period ended 31 March 2017 have been delivered to the Registrar of Companies; the auditor’s report on those accounts
was not qualified, did not include a reference to any matters to which the auditor’s drew attention by the way of
emphasis without qualifying the report and did not contain statements under section 498 (2) or (3) of the Companies
Act 2006. To date, not statutory financial statements have been filed for any period ending later than 31 March 2017.
