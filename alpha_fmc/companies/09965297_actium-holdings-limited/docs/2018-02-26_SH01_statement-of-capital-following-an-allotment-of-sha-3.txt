Statement of capital (prescribed particulars of rights attached to shares)

Class of share

REDEEMABLE DEFERRED SHARES

Prescribed particulars

(A) The holders of Redeemable Deferred shares
shall not be entitled to attend or vote at any
general meeting of the Company.

(B) No dividend may be paid in respect of any
Redeemable Deferred Share.

(C) The holders of Redeemable Deferred Shares
shall not be entitled to receive, on a return of
capital on liquidation or otherwise, the surplus
assets of the Company remaining after payment of
its liabilities.

(D) Subject to the 2006 Act and of every other
statute for the time being in force concerning
companies and affecting the Company, the Company
shall have the right at any time to redeem the
whole or any number of the Redeemable Deferred
Shares in issue.

The Company shall pay in respect of each Redeemable
Deferred Share redeemed the nominal value of the
Redeemable Deferred Share.

On the due date, the Company shall pay to each
registered holder of Redeemable Deferred Shares the
amount payable in respect of such redemption. On
receipt of that amount, each such holder shall
surrender to the Company the certificate for the
shares that are to be redeemed (or an indemnity in
a form reasonably satisfactory to the Board in
respect of any lost share certificate) to be
cancelled. If any certificate (or indemnity) so
surrendered includes any shares that are not
redeemable at that time, the Company shall issue a
new share certificate for the balance of the shares
not redeemable to the holder. If there is more than
one holder of Redeemable Deferred Shares, any
redemption shall be made among such holders pro
rata (as nearly as possible) to their respective
holdings of Redeemable Deferred Shares.
