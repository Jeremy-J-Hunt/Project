CAPITAL

On a return of capital, a winding-up or liquidation
of the Company or any other return of capital {each

expenses incurred 1n connection with such Relevant
Event will be distributed amongst the shareholders
as follows

Firstly, 1n paying to the holders of the C Ordinary

amount paid up (or credited as paid up) in respect
of the nominal value and any share premium paid for
such Shares, and

Secondly, the balance to be paid to the holders of
the Al Ordinary Shares, the A2 Ordinary Shares and
the B Ordinary Shares in proportion to the number
of Al Ordinary Shares, A2 Ordinary Shares and B
Ordinary Shares held by them respectively (pari
