BACKGROUND

A. The Vendor is the registered holder and beneficial owner of the Sale Shares. The Company
wishes to purchase and the Vendor wishes to sell the Sale Shares on the terms of this
agreement.

passed prior to the date of this agreement in accordance with the procedure in chapter 2 of
part 13 of the Companies Act 2006. An unexecuted copy of this agreement was sent or
submitted to every eligible member at or before the time at which such special written
resolution was sent or submitted to them.

Cc. Each member of the Company has waived any right he, she or it may have under the articles
of association of the Company or otherwise to have any of the Sale Shares offered to him, her
or it for purchase prior to them being purchased by the Company under this agreement.

the Company for all purposes.

Shareholder") are parties to this agreement for the purposes of clause 7.5 only and for no
