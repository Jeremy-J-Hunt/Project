Details of variation of rights

Please give details of the variation of rights attached to shares.

Variation

In accordance with Section 630 of the Companies Act 2006 and
article 45 of the Company’s articles of association, the holders of
the A Ordinary Shares of £0.01 each in the capital of the Company
(“Shares”) consented by written special resolution to the variation,
modification or abrogation of the rights, privileges and restrictions
attaching to the Shares that will or may be effected thereby, as a
result of the re-designation of 92,815 A Ordinary Shares of £0.01
each in the capital of the Company as 92,815 B Ordinary Shares of
£0.01 each in the capital of the Company (the Re-Designation).
The rights of the A Ordinary and B Ordinary share classes remain
unchanged, as set out in the Articles of Association.
