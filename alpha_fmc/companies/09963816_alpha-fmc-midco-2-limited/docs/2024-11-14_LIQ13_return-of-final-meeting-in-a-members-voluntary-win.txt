Progress in the Liquidations

Asset realisations

The receipts and payments accounts attached at Appendix C details the asset realisations in the
liquidations.

As detailed in the statement of assets and liabilities in the Declarations of Solvency, the sole assets of the
Companies were intercompany debtors totalling £1 each. As noted below, the intercompany debtors were
distributed in specie to the Companies’ respective members on 15 October 2024.

No other assets have been identified or realised during the course of the liquidations.

HMRC

Following the commencement of the liquidations, HMRC were notified of the proceedings.

With regards to corporation tax (‘CT’), the Liquidators liaised with the Companies’ incumbent tax advisers
bringing the Companies’ tax affairs up to date in respect of the final pre-liquidation periods. All liabilities
due to HMRC in respect of CT relate to the pre-liquidation periods and were settled prior to the
Liquidators’ appointments.

The Companies were not employing entities and were not registered for PAYE.

The Companies were historically registered for VAT and, following the Liquidators’ appointment, the
Companies were removed from the VAT Group. As detailed below, a claim was received from HMRC in
respect of VAT and the liability has now been settled in full.

Following settlement of the VAT liability, the Liquidators have received confirmation from HMRC that they
have no further claims in the liquidations.

Creditors
There were no known creditors in the liquidations.
Following the appointment of the Liquidators, statutory advertisements were placed in the London

Gazette and Times newspaper inviting creditors to submit claims. A notice of intended dividend was sent
with a last date of proving of 1 July 2024.

A claim was received from HMRC in relation to the group registration for VAT. The claim was admitted in
the liquidations and, as there are no funds held, the liability was settled by an associated group company.

Return on capital
As detailed above, at the date of the Liquidators’ appointments, the sole asset of each Company consisted
of a £1 intercompany debtor.

On 15 October 2024, the Companies receivables were distributed in specie to the member, Alpha Financial
Markets Consulting Plc.

Other matters

In addition to the above matters, we have dealt with all statutory matters required by legislation and
administrative work incidental to our duties as Liquidators.

Appendix B

Members’ rights

Within 21 days of receipt of the Proposed Final Account, members with at least 5% in value of the total
voting rights of all members having the right to vote at general meetings of a Company or any member
with the permission of court, may request in writing that the Joint Liquidators provide further information
about their remuneration or expenses which have been itemised in the Proposed Final Account.

Within 14 days of receipt of the request, the Joint Liquidators must provide all of the information asked
for, unless they think that:

e the time or cost in preparing the information would be excessive, or

e disclosure of the information would be prejudicial to the conduct of a liquidation or might reasonably
be expected to lead to violence against any person, or

e — they are subject to confidentiality obligations in respect of the information.

The Joint Liquidators are also required to give reasons for not providing all of the requested information.

Members with at least 10% in value of the total voting rights of all members having the right to vote at
general meetings of a Company may, within eight weeks of receipt of the Proposed Final Account, make an
application to court that the basis fixed for the Joint Liquidators’ remuneration, the remuneration
charged, or the expenses incurred by the Joint Liquidators, as set out in the Proposed Final Account, are
excessive.

The Joint Liquidators may conclude that the affairs of a Company are fully wound up before 3 January
2025 if every member confirms in writing to us that they do not intend to make any request for further
information about remuneration or expenses or a court application.

When the affairs of a Company are fully wound up the Joint Liquidators will make up the Final Account
and deliver it to the members, When the Final Account is delivered to the Registrar of Companies the
Joint Liquidators will be released under Section 171(6) of the Act.

Members may access a copy of BDO LLP’s charging and expenses policy at https: //www.bdo.co.uk/en-
gb/insights/advisory/business-restructuring/creditors-guides.

The Insolvency Service has established a central gateway for considering complaints in respect of
Insolvency Practitioners. In the event that you make a complaint to us but are not satisfied with the
response, then you should visit https: //www.gov.uk/complain-about-insolvency-practitioner where you
will find further information on how you may pursue the complaint.

The Joint Liquidators are bound by the Insolvency Code of Ethics when carrying out all professional work
relating to this Liquidation. A copy of the code can be found at
https: //www.icaew.com/technical/ethics/icaew-code-of-ethics/icaew-code-of-ethics.
